# SMAIT Al Kahfi website redesign

Approved by the user on 17 September 2026. Deliver one self-contained repository in `Pemrograman-Web/Tugas-2`, using HTML, native CSS, and JavaScript. Keep the original homepage snapshot at `school-source.html` in this directory.

## Design

An editorial school website for prospective parents, students, current students, and alumni. White backgrounds, deep blue accent, Outfit typography, authentic school photography, compact diagrams, and generous but practical spacing. Motion supports orientation and interaction, with reduced-motion alternatives. Avoid fabricated statistics, stock photographs, unnecessary decoration, and scroll hijacking. The school audience and approved restrained direction take precedence over experimental defaults in design skills.
